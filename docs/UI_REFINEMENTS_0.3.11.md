# UI refinements — 0.3.11-alpha

- The **View** selector now floats over the upper-right corner of the result viewport and no longer consumes a separate layout row.
- Thumbnail views use pixel scrolling and an accelerated wheel/scrollbar step.
- The Models tab presents EmbeddingGemma and OCR in separate rows, each with **Download** and **Verify files** actions on the right.
- The footer menu is named **Information**.
- The redundant global image-clear button was removed; every attached image retains its individual red remove button.
- The Vision checkbox and its explanation share one compact line on the Settings tab.
